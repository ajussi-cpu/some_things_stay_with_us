Some Things Stay With Us

Some Things Stay With Us is a personal blog about past relationships, memories, regrets, and the experiences that stay with us even after they are over, it talks about the moments we often think about, the words we never got to say, the feelings we struggled to explain, and the conversations we wish had gone differently

Not every story ends with a clear answer, and not every feeling is easy to put into words. Sometimes, misunderstandings happen because we fail to say what we really mean or because we don't fully understand each other. These small moments can leave a lasting mark, making it harder to forget certain people and experiences

This blog is also about learning to accept the past. Moving forward does not mean forgetting everything or pretending that nothing happened. It means understanding our mistakes, learning from what we have been through, and accepting that some things are beyond our control

This project was created as part of my web development coursework using HTML and CSS. It features a simple, responsive, and minimalist design that reflects the personal and thoughtful message of the blog
